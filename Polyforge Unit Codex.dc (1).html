Reading the map

Silhouette carries the unit, color and headgear carry the tribe. At the map's 72×36 tile the head shape and weapon angle survive; interior detail does not, so every kit puts its identity above the shoulders and out at the weapon tip.

Held constant

Ground shadow, ink outline weight, veteran star, damage bar and the acted-dim overlay are shared across all thirty-two builds, so state reads identically whoever you play.

In engine

All builds live in one drawing module keyed by tribe kit × unit type, called where drawUnit() in src/render/renderer.ts draws its circle-and-head placeholder today. See the game screen for it in situ.
